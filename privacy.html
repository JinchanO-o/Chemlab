Privacy

Anonymous study progress is stored locally in your browser under namespaced chemistry keys.

When Supabase is configured and you sign in, quiz history, flashcard progress, and derived statistics may be synchronized to your account. Comments are associated with your account and element symbol.

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by this site. The browser client uses only the public anon key and relies on Supabase Row Level Security.
